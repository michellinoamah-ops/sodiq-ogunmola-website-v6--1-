Getting started

This dashboard controls three things on the public site.

  • Messages sent from the Contact page appear under Messages.
  • Comments submitted on any article appear under Editorial Review as pending until you approve or reject them. Approved comments appear publicly right away.
  • Anything you add under Publications replaces the built-in starter list shown on the public Publications page and homepage.

New here? Open the Publications tab and click "Import starter list" to load the eleven existing papers into the database, then edit them freely.
